Overview

ToneSeeker is a comprehensive library dedicated to guitar legends, their signature gear, and amplifier settings. Musicians and guitar enthusiasts can explore detailed profiles of famous guitarists, discover the specific amps, pedals, and equipment they use, and learn the stories behind their iconic tones. It's a one-stop resource for understanding how legendary players shaped their sound.

Key Features

  • Guitarist Profiles: Detailed bios and lore about famous guitarists and their musical evolution.

  • Gear & Amp Database: Comprehensive listings of amps, pedals, guitars, and settings used by legends.

  • Tone Breakdowns: In-depth analysis of how gear and settings combine to create iconic tones.

My Role

As the developer of ToneSeeker, I built the entire web application to serve as a searchable, user-friendly library for guitar enthusiasts. My focus was on organizing complex data (guitarist profiles, gear specs, amp settings) into an intuitive interface. I structured the database in Firebase, designed responsive components for browsing and filtering gear information, and created detailed profile pages that tell the story behind each legend's tone.
