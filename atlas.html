Overview

Atlas is a comprehensive mobile fitness application designed to provide users with intelligent workout routines and granular nutrition tracking. By leveraging AI, it generates individualized workout plans adapted to a user's biological metrics, goals, and equipment availability, cultivating a robust fitness community right at their fingertips.

Key Features

  • AI Workout Generation: Dynamic planning based on user feedback and progress.

  • Nutrition Tracking: Macro and micro-nutrient logging via manual input.

  • Community Hub: Social feeds, challenges, and leaderboards to keep users engaged.

My Role

As the lead developer on this student project, I was responsible for architecting the backend services and integrating the AI engine. I also collaborated deeply on the front-end components, ensuring a snappy, fluid mobile experience across platforms.
